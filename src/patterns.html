文型

The frames a Japanese sentence is built from: which slot each particle marks, how the frame changes for past, negative and polite, and how to turn it into a question. Grouped by grammar rather than by situation, so you can see one pattern in all its forms. Each frame links to its explanation on the grammar page and to real sentences in the sentence builder.

The frames and notes here are this site\'s own work and have not been checked by a native speaker. Use them as a map of the grammar, and your course for the full explanation.
