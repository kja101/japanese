言葉

Every word the site knows, in one list: __COUNT__ words from N5 to N2, with their readings, meanings and an example sentence where there is one. Sort them by type or in kana order, and tap any kanji to open its card in the kanji deck. Words written only in kana also have their own pages: kana words and katakana words.

Words

Word levels come from the open JLPT word lists (derived from tanos.co.uk); JLPT has published no official lists since 2010, so they are a guide. Types are worked out from each word and its English, so a few will sit in the wrong group. Examples come from this site\'s own sentences.
