Spaced mode splits words and particles to make them easier to see. Natural mode shows how Japanese is normally written, without spaces. Hiragana mode turns kanji into hiragana and leaves katakana loanwords as they are. Romaji uses Hepburn with long vowels written out (arigatou, koohii). Phrases marked "You'll hear this" are what staff are likely to say to you. In test mode, tap a hidden line to reveal it.
