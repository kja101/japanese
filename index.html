日本語

Free, open study tools for Japanese, from kana to the N2 kanji. Every page runs in the browser, saves your progress on your own device, can read the Japanese aloud (tap ▶ next to any word or sentence), works offline once you've opened it (add it to your home screen), and uses the same colours for readings: indigo for on readings (from Chinese), green for kun readings (native Japanese), red dotted for special whole-word readings.

Kanji

Three pages, three jobs: look a kanji up, learn them day by day, or see them in use.

マスター漢字Master kanji shapes, N5 to N2 Look up and exploreThe reference for every kanji, all 1,104 from N5 to N2, with a level switch. Each card has the parts and a story, look-alikes, and every word by on and kun reading, linked to the other kanji in it. Browse by shape family, by sound (including the parts that give the sound) or by topic, test yourself with flashcards, and write your own stories. 学習Daily study plan, N5 to N2 Learn day by dayHow to learn kanji fastest, and spaced-repetition flashcards that decide what to review each day: N5 and N4 first, then on through N3 and N2 if you choose, parts before the kanji built from them, and optional word cards from the 4,280-word list. Your progress and your own stories are shared with the master deck. 場面Kanji by situation See them in use200 N4 kanji in 13 real-life situations, from the restaurant to the pharmacy, with 1,465 example sentences, where you'll see each one, and N5 to N2 words.

Phrases and sentences

会話帳Interactive phrasebook Start here474 phrases in 14 situations, linked to 1,465 sentences in the sentence builder, from greetings to emergencies. Tap a word to drop it into every slot, switch on grammar blocks to see how each sentence is built, and star the ones you want in My phrasebook. 言葉Words, N5 to N2The whole vocabulary in one place: 4,280 words with readings, meanings, audio and an example sentence where there is one. Sort by type or in kana order, filter by level, and tap any kanji to open its card. 文型Sentence patternsStart with the particle map: each slot of a sentence and the two or three markers that can follow it. Then 55 frames by grammar type: the same frame in every tense, how to turn it into a question, and links to the grammar page and real sentences. 文法Grammar, N5 to N2112 patterns that turn words into sentences, from ～たい and ～ている to the passive, keigo, ～わけにはいかない and ～ざるを得ない, with a level switch. What each one means, how to form it, and examples you can listen to. 文Sentence builderThe grammar lesson behind the phrasebook: 1,465 sentences in 13 situations with a level switch (245 at N5, 552 at N4, 1,465 at N2), the Japanese and English order side by side, plus every N5 and N4 verb and adjective by type with its ます, て and ない forms.

Kana

かなの言葉Kana words454 words you write in hiragana rather than kanji, N5 to N2, grouped by use: greetings, question and pointing words, linking words, adverbs, sound words, verbs and everyday nouns, with example sentences. カタカナの言葉Katakana words353 loanwords, N5 to N2, by topic: food, clothes, home, travel, work, sport and more. Notes flag the false friends (マンション, コンセント) and words from languages other than English, with example sentences. かなKana and their soundsEvery kana with the signs built from it, the signs and spellings that sound the same, and words that share a sound, with pitch patterns where they tell words apart.

Where to begin

New to kanji: do the daily study plan, and open any kanji in the master deck when you want more on it. Past N4 or revising: use the master deck's level switch and flashcards. For speaking and reading in context: the phrasebook and the situation page.

Data sources and licences are in the repository README. JLPT has not published official lists since 2010, so every level here is a guide.
