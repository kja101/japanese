日本語

Free, open study tools for Japanese, from first kana to the N2 kanji. Everything runs in the browser, works offline once loaded, and saves your progress on your own device.

Kanji

マスター漢字Master kanji shapes, N5 to N2 Start hereAll 981 kanji in shape families, with a level switch. Stories, look-alikes, on and kun readings with real words, sound maps and flashcards. 学習Learn N5 and N4 with spaced repetitionThe method explained, plus flashcards that schedule every review for you. 10 new kanji a day. 漢字N5 and N4 by shape, sound and topicThe same 284 kanji sorted three ways, including the parts that give away the sound. 場面Kanji by situationN4 kanji grouped by where you meet them: the restaurant, the station, the pharmacy. 基本Master kanji shapes, N5 and N4 onlyThe earlier, lighter edition of the shapes deck.

Words and sentences

単語Vocabulary sheetsCommon nouns, verbs and adjectives by topic, grouped by shared kanji. フレーズPhrase guideTravel and everyday phrases as frames: tap a word and it drops into every slot. 文Sentence builder120 sentences broken into colour-coded blocks, so you can see how Japanese word order works.

Kana

かなKana soundsEvery sign and the signs built from it, the ones that sound the same, and words that share a sound.

Readings are colour-coded the same way everywhere: indigo for on readings (from Chinese), green for kun readings (native Japanese), red dotted for special whole-word readings.

Data sources and licences are listed in the repository README. JLPT has not published official lists since 2010, so every level here is a guide.
