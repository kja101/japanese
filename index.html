日本語

Free, open study tools for Japanese, from kana to the N2 kanji. Every page runs in the browser, saves your progress on your own device, and uses the same colours for readings: indigo for on readings (from Chinese), green for kun readings (native Japanese), red dotted for special whole-word readings.

Kanji

マスター漢字Master kanji shapes, N5 to N2 Start hereThe main deck. All 981 kanji from N5 to N2 in 61 shape families, with a level switch. Each card has the parts and a story, look-alikes, and every word by on and kun reading, with compounds linked to each kanji in them. Sound maps, a sound index, flashcards, and space for your own stories. 学習Daily study plan with spaced repetition, N5 and N4How to learn kanji fastest, and flashcards that schedule every review for you: 284 kanji in 29 lessons of 10, parts before the kanji built from them. 場面Kanji by situation, with sentences200 N4 kanji in 13 real-life situations, from the restaurant to the pharmacy. 222 example sentences, where you'll see each kanji, and N5 to N2 words for every card. 形・音・話題N5 and N4 kanji by shape, sound and topicThe 284 kanji sorted three ways, including the parts that give away the sound (寺 gives ジ in 時 and 持). Every card lists its N5 to N2 words by reading. 基本Master kanji shapes, N5 and N4 classic editionThe earlier, lighter shapes deck: 284 kanji with N5 and N4 words only. Superseded by the N5 to N2 deck above.

Phrases and sentences

会話帳Interactive phrasebook Start hereAbout 500 phrases and sentences in 14 situations, from greetings to emergencies. Tap a word to drop it into every slot, switch on grammar blocks to see how each sentence is built, and star the ones you want in My phrasebook. 文Sentence builderThe grammar lesson behind the phrasebook: 120 sentences in colour-coded blocks, with the block system explained and the Japanese and English order side by side. 単語Vocabulary sheetsAbout 390 common nouns, verbs and adjectives across 11 topics, grouped by shared kanji so you can see how each character builds its words.

Kana

かなKana and their soundsEvery kana with the signs built from it, the signs and spellings that sound the same, and words that share a sound, with pitch patterns where they tell words apart.

Where to begin

New to kanji: follow the daily study plan, and read each day's kanji in the master deck. Revising or going past N4: use the master deck's level switch. For speaking and real-life reading, use the phrasebook; the situation page shows the same sentences next to the kanji they use.

Data sources and licences are in the repository README. JLPT has not published official lists since 2010, so every level here is a guide.
