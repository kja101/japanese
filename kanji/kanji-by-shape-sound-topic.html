漢字 N5・N4

All 284 kanji for JLPT N5 and N4, seen three ways: grouped by the shape they are built from, by the sound they make, and by topic. Each card shows the on reading (from Chinese, in katakana), the kun reading (native Japanese, in hiragana) and a common word, with each kanji in the word linked to its own card and labelled with the reading it takes there.

Kun readings show okurigana (the kana that follow the kanji) in a lighter tone: た.べる means the kanji is read た and followed by べる. Only the most common readings are listed. JLPT has not published official kanji lists since 2010, so the N5 and N4 split follows the widely used community lists. In test mode, tap a card to reveal it.
