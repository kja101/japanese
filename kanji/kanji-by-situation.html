場面の漢字

The N4 kanji sorted by where you'll actually meet them: the restaurant, the bar, the pharmacy, the station, the office. Each situation has example sentences, then its kanji as cards with their parts, a story, where you'll see them, and every word that uses them from N5 up to N2.
