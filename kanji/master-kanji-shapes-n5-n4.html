マスター漢字

Master Kanji Shapes, the complete N5 and N4 deck. All 284 kanji in 53 chapters, built Heisig's way: learn the shape, learn what it gets confused with, then learn its readings through real words, split into on (from Chinese) and kun (native Japanese). Each chapter opens with a sound map of the on readings its kanji share, and the Sound index groups every shared on reading across the deck. Tier 1's twelve master shapes come first, covering the largest families, then the smaller families and the kanji that stand on their own.
